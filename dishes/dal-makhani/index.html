Calories in Dal Makhani (1 bowl (200ml))

MasalaFit’s Dal Makhani entry reflects a standard North Indian / Pakistani serving of 1 bowl (200ml). Compare your plate with the range below, especially when butter and cream differs from usual. A common variation is Amritsari Dal Makhani (+30 cal). You may also see it described as daal makhani, dhal makhani, dahl makhani, makhani dal, and makhani daal.
