Calories in Puttu / Pittu (2 cylinders (150g))

Puttu / Pittu can vary substantially from kitchen to kitchen, so this page begins with 2 cylinders (150g). The listed range helps account for differences such as grated coconut layered inside. A common variation is Wheat Puttu (~240 cal). You may also see it described as puttu, pittu, rice puttu, kuzha puttu, and sri lankan pittu.
