Calories in Chai (1 cup (200ml))

This guide uses 1 cup (200ml) of Chai as a practical logging reference. In this recipe, sugar added is one of the details most likely to move the final calorie number. A common variation is No sugar with low-fat milk (~40 cal). You may also see it described as tea, chai tea, masala chai, masala tea, and indian tea.

What drives the calories

  • Sugar added
  • Full-fat milk vs low-fat milk
  • Condensed milk or extra milk solids
  • Full-fat milk ratio very milk-heavy
  • Sugar 2-3 tsp per cup
  • Number of cups per day multiplier
  • Full fat milk amount
  • Spices negligible

Variations

  • No sugar with low-fat milk (~40 cal)
  • Very sweet full-fat milk chai with condensed milk (~150 cal)
  • Peshawari Qehwa green tea (~30 cal)
  • Kashmiri Pink Chai Noon Chai (~150 cal)
  • No sugar version (~45 kcal)
  • Very sweet milky (~140 kcal)
  • Black tea no milk (~5 kcal)
